main_message_paragraphs:
  - "Living out God&rsquo;s Word not only reveals genuine faith but leads to a rooted and anchored life that can withstand the inevitable storms of life. The wise builder hears and obeys Jesus, building on the solid foundation of a relationship with Him&mdash;dependence, trust, and intimacy that produces lasting stability. In contrast, the foolish builder may hear the same words but does not live them out, revealing a lack of true faith. Though their life may appear similar for a time, when the storms come, their lack of foundation is exposed, and what once seemed secure collapses with devastating force. Ultimately, every life is tested, and only what is built on Jesus will stand."

dt_intro_paragraphs:
  - "Jesus ends the Sermon on the Mount with a decision, not more teaching. After covering identity, integrity, trust, and righteousness, He brings everything down to one question: Will you act on what you&rsquo;ve heard? This parable is not about knowledge&mdash;it&rsquo;s about response. The weight is not in what a man knows, but in what he does with it."

    title: "The Wise Builder"
    body: "The wise builder hears the truth and acts on it. He builds on the rock, which means he does the slow, intentional work of creating a genuine relationship with Jesus. His obedience is steady and consistent, not rushed or reactive. It is not impressive in the moment, but over time, it becomes a strength that holds. His life is anchored because he follows through on what Jesus says."
  - eyebrow: "02"
    title: "The Foolish Builder"
    body: "The foolish builder hears the same truth but does nothing with it. On the surface, his life can look just as strong. The structure still goes up, and everything appears stable. But underneath, there is no foundation. This is the danger Jesus exposes&mdash;a man can look solid for a long time and still not be solid. When truth is not acted on, it creates a false sense of security."
  - eyebrow: "03"
    title: "The Storm"
    body: "The storm represents pressure that every man will face&mdash;adversity, failure, loss, and exposure. It does not create a man&rsquo;s foundation; it reveals it. When pressure hits, what is built beneath the surface becomes visible. What looked strong either holds or collapses. This is the moment where there is no hiding what a man has built his life on."
dt_closing_paragraphs:
  - "Jesus is not just a teacher to listen to&mdash;He is Lord to be followed. Through Him, men are brought into a relationship with God, and that relationship is revealed through obedience over time. The cross makes that relationship possible, and obedience becomes the evidence that it is real. A life built on Jesus is not formed through agreement, but through surrender. The question is not whether you hear the truth, but whether you follow Him."

  - "Pressure doesn&rsquo;t create your foundation&mdash;it exposes whether your life is built on surrender to Jesus or surface-level agreement."

personal_reflection_paragraphs:
  - "Most men don&rsquo;t lack knowledge&mdash;they lack follow-through. You know what matters. You&rsquo;ve heard the truth, felt conviction, and seen what needs to change. But if you don&rsquo;t act, nothing is being built. That&rsquo;s the trap. You can agree with everything and still walk away unchanged. Over time, that builds a life that may look strong on the outside but has no foundation. Real strength is built in the unseen&mdash;daily obedience, dependence on God, doing what&rsquo;s right when no one is watching. When trials and pressures come, your true foundation is exposed. And when a man realizes he has built his life on a weak foundation, it can feel devastating. Jesus says the house &lsquo;fell with a great crash&rsquo; because a collapse is not small or painless. It can leave a man feeling broken, ashamed, empty, and wondering if everything he built was meaningless. But that warning is also mercy. Jesus exposes weak foundations before it is too late, inviting men to rebuild their lives on something solid and lasting."
