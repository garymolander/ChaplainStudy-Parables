  - "The condition of our heart and how we hear and receive the Word of God dictates the spiritual fruitfulness of our lives and our intimacy with God. The message of the Gospel is powerful and unchanging, but it does not impact every man the same way."
  - "Some hear it and remain unchanged, while others are transformed over time. The difference is not the message — it's the condition of the heart receiving it. Spiritual growth, consistency, and fruitfulness are always rooted in what is happening internally."

dt_intro_paragraphs:
  - "Jesus tells this parable to a large, mixed crowd — people with different levels of interest, belief, and commitment. The same message is going out to all of them, but it lands differently depending on the condition of the listener."
  - "Using a familiar farming image, Jesus explains that the seed is the Word of God — the message of salvation. The message is perfect and powerful, able to transform any man through Jesus' life, death, and resurrection. If the seed is good, the issue is not the message — it's the soil it lands on."

cards:
  - eyebrow: "01 · The Hard Path"
    title: "Closed Heart"
    variant: "soil-hard"
    body: "The hard path represents a closed heart. Truth is heard, but it never penetrates. Pride, pain, or unbelief keep it at the surface, and it is quickly taken away before it can take root. A man can be around truth often and still remain unchanged if his heart is not open."
  - eyebrow: "02 · The Rocky Soil"
    title: "Shallow Heart"
    variant: "soil-rocky"
    body: "The rocky soil represents a shallow heart. There is an initial response — interest, emotion, even excitement — but no depth. When pressure, adversity, or challenge comes, the response fades because there are no roots. Without depth, there is no endurance."
  - eyebrow: "03 · The Thorny Soil"
    title: "Divided Heart"
    variant: "soil-thorny"
    body: "The thorny soil represents a divided heart. Growth begins, but it gets choked out by competing priorities — success, money, comfort, pressure, and distraction. The issue is not rejection of truth, but competition with it. Over time, what matters most gets crowded out."
  - eyebrow: "04 · The Good Soil"
    title: "Surrendered Heart"
    variant: "soil-good"
    body: "The good soil represents a receptive and surrendered heart. It is not perfect, but it is open. It hears the Word, holds onto it, and remains steady over time, producing real fruit. Jesus makes it clear that not every man who hears the Word produces fruit. The difference is not ability or opportunity — it is surrender."

key_points:
  - "The issue is not the message, it's the condition of your heart — the same truth produces different results based on what it lands on."
  - "Shallow, hard, or divided hearts prevent real growth, while a surrendered heart allows truth to take root and produce fruit."
  - "Transformation comes when a man receives, holds onto, and lives out God's Word — not just when he hears it."

  - "Good soil is different. It does not need to be perfect soil, but it is required to be surrendered soil. It listens, holds onto truth, and obeys over time. That is where real growth happens."
