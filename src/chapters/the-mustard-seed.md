dt_intro_paragraphs:
  - "Jesus tells this parable to redefine what the Kingdom of God actually looks like. To the men watching Him, nothing about His ministry seemed impressive&mdash;no power, no platform, no visible momentum. That disconnect is the point. God&rsquo;s kingdom does not begin with what looks strong or significant on the surface. It begins in ways that are easy to dismiss, forcing men to trust what God is doing beneath what they can see."

This is how God often moves&mdash;quietly, without drawing attention, and without immediate results. Jesus Himself reflects this pattern. His life did not match expectations, and from a human perspective, it looked insignificant and even like a failure. But once something is planted in God&rsquo;s hands, it does not stop growing."

This is how God works&mdash;not through immediate results, but through steady, unstoppable growth that develops beneath the surface before it is ever seen."
dt_closing_paragraphs:
  - "God&rsquo;s kingdom grows through faithfulness, not visibility. Through Jesus, what looked like weakness and failure&mdash;His life, death, and crucifixion&mdash;was actually the beginning of something unstoppable. The cross did not look like victory, but it established the kingdom that continues to grow. This is how God works. He builds through small, unseen obedience that leads to lasting impact. The question is not whether God is working, but whether a man will trust Him enough to stay faithful in what feels small."
