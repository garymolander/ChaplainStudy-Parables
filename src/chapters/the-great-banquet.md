main_message_paragraphs:
  - "The kingdom of God has arrived in the person of Jesus, and those who would have been most expected to recognize and receive it &ndash; the religious elite &ndash; ultimately reject the invitation, while those least expected to enter &ndash; the poor, the outcast, and the morally disqualified &ndash; respond with repentance and faith. Jesus reveals that status is meaningless in God&rsquo;s kingdom, His invitation (of Himself and His kingdom) extends to anyone who will receive it, and the kingdom is not filled by those of high status, the self-assured, or those confident in their own righteousness, but by the humble and willing."

dt_intro_paragraphs:
  - "Jesus tells this parable in a room full of religious men who assumed they were already included in God&rsquo;s kingdom. They built their identity on status, effort, and obedience to God&rsquo;s law. When someone confidently speaks about being part of the future feast, Jesus responds with this story to expose a deeper issue&mdash;assumption without response. The invitation is real and available, but belonging to God is not about proximity or appearance; it is about whether a man actually responds when the invitation is given."

cards:
  - eyebrow: "01"
    title: "The Invited Guests"
    body: "The invited guests represent men who assume they belong but fail to respond. They have already received the invitation and even accepted it at one point, but when the time comes to act, they choose something else. Work, possessions, and relationships take priority. None of these is wrong, which is what makes them dangerous. Their excuses sound reasonable to us, but they reveal how preoccupied they were with lesser things compared to the extravagant invitation they received."
  - eyebrow: "02"
    title: "The Outsiders"
    body: "The outsiders represent men who would never expect to be included. They are overlooked, pushed to the margins, and considered unlikely candidates. Yet when the invitation expands, they respond. This reveals that status, effort, and background do not earn a place in God&rsquo;s kingdom. The only thing that matters is whether a man receives what is offered. Those who thought they were missing it, while those who never expected it step into it."
  - eyebrow: "03"
    title: "The Master"
    body: "The Master represents God&mdash;fully prepared, fully committed, and extending the invitation completely. Through Jesus, everything needed for a man to be brought into a relationship with God has already been accomplished. The cost has been paid, and the invitation is open. The issue is not whether God has done enough&mdash;it is whether a man will respond. The Kingdom of God is not something to acknowledge later; it is something to step into now."
