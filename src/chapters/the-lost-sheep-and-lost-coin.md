dt_closing_paragraphs:
  - "These parables reveal the heart of God. He does not wait for men to fix themselves — He moves toward them. That pursuit ultimately leads to Jesus, who came to seek and save the lost and gave His life to bring men back into a relationship with God."
  - "Every man apart from Him is lost, whether it is obvious or hidden behind success and discipline. But when a man is found — when he turns back to God — there is celebration. Heaven rejoices. The question is not whether God pursues, but whether a man will recognize his need to be found and respond to that pursuit."
key_points:

  - "The real question is not whether God is pursuing you, but whether you will recognize your need and respond to be found."
